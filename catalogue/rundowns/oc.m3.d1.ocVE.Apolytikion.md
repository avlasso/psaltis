# Εὐφραινέσθω τὰ οὐράνια

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Third Mode.

**What it says.** Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad, for the Lord has worked might with His arm: He trampled death by death and became the first-born of the dead; He delivered us from the belly of Hades and granted the world great mercy. Psalm 96 in the opening, Colossians and 1 Corinthians behind the middle.

**Mode.** Third Mode, enharmonic, from Ga. Bold and open; the opening *Εὐφραινέσθω* climbs straight up and sets the tone.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
