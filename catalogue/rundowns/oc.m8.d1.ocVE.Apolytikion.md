# Ἐξ ὕψους κατῆλθες ὁ εὔσπλαγχνος

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Plagal Fourth Mode.

**What it says.** You came down from on high, O compassionate One; You accepted the three-day burial so as to free us from the passions. *Our life and our resurrection, Lord, glory to You.* The shortest of the eight; two sentences.

**Mode.** Plagal Fourth, diatonic, from Ni. Calm and even; the everyday mode. Because the text is short, the melody carries more weight per word — get the cadences right.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
