# Νεανές — apēchēma of the Second Mode

**What it is.** The intonation formula of the Second Mode: a short melodic figure on the syllables *Ne-a-nés*, sung by the chanter (often under the breath) before a hymn to fix the mode's base note and character in the ear. The syllables mean nothing; they descend from the ancient *enēchēmata* and have been passed down with the modes.

**When and where.** Before the first hymn of a group in this mode — after the reader announces "Ἦχος …" at Vespers or Orthros — and whenever you need to find the mode again. At home: sing it before every hymn in this mode until the hymn's opening comes on its own.

**Character.** Soft chromatic, tender and sweet; suited to petition and to the Theotokos.
