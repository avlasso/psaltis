# Ὅτε κατῆλθες πρὸς τὸν θάνατον

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Second Mode.

**What it says.** When You went down to death, You who are Life immortal, then You slew Hades with the lightning-flash of Your divinity; and when You raised the dead from the depths, all the heavenly powers cried out: *Giver of life, Christ our God, glory to You.* One long sentence, so breathe at the commas.

**Mode.** Second Mode, soft chromatic, from Di. Tender; the descending phrases on *θάνατον* and *Ἅδην* are where the mode's colour shows.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
