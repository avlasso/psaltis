# Τὸν συνάναρχον Λόγον

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Plagal First Mode. An *automelon*; many other apolytikia are sung to this melody.

**What it says.** Let us, the faithful, praise and worship the Word, co-eternal with the Father and the Spirit, born of the Virgin for our salvation; for He willed to be lifted up on the Cross in the flesh, to endure death, and to raise the dead by His glorious resurrection. A creed in miniature: Trinity, Incarnation, Cross, Resurrection.

**Mode.** Plagal First, diatonic, from Pa (heirmologic from Ke). Sweet with an undertone of sorrow; the tune is one of the best known in the whole repertoire.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
