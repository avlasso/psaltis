# Κατέλυσας τῷ Σταυρῷ σου τὸν θάνατον

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Grave Mode.

**What it says.** By Your Cross You destroyed death; You opened Paradise to the thief; You turned the lament of the myrrh-bearers into joy, and You commanded Your apostles to preach that You are risen, O Christ God, granting the world great mercy. Four scenes in four clauses: Cross, thief, myrrh-bearers, apostles.

**Mode.** Grave Mode, enharmonic, from Ga low. Weighty and settled — "barys" means heavy; it does not climb far and rests often on its base.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
