# Ἀγγελικαὶ δυνάμεις ἐπὶ τὸ μνῆμά σου

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Plagal Second Mode. An *automelon*.

**What it says.** Angelic powers were at Your tomb and the guards became as dead; Mary stood by the grave seeking Your immaculate body. You despoiled Hades and were not touched by it; You met the Virgin, granting life. *Lord, risen from the dead, glory to You.* Matthew 28 in the first line, the Harrowing of Hades in the middle.

**Mode.** Plagal Second, hard chromatic, from Pa. The most mournful of the modes, here carrying a resurrection text — sing it grave, not glum.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
