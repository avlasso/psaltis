# Τὸ φαιδρὸν τῆς ἀναστάσεως κήρυγμα

**What it is.** The Resurrectional apolytikion of the Fourth Mode.

**What it says.** The women disciples of the Lord learned the joyful message of the resurrection from the angel and cast off the ancestral sentence; boasting, they said to the apostles: *Death is despoiled; Christ God is risen, granting the world great mercy.* The myrrh-bearers become the first preachers.

**Mode.** Fourth Mode, diatonic, from Vou (Legetos branch in the heirmologic apolytikion). Festive and light on its feet; keep it moving.

**When and where.** This is the week's Resurrectional apolytikion, so it comes round every eighth week. Saturday Great Vespers: after *Now let your servant depart* and the Trisagion prayers, just before the dismissal, followed by its Theotokion. Sunday Orthros: after *God is the Lord*, sung twice, then the Theotokion. Divine Liturgy: after the Small Entrance, before the saints' apolytikia and the kontakion. It is the hymn a congregation is most likely to sing along with.
