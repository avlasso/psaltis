# 10 — *Where do I start?* and roadmap signposting

## Outcome

A help page answers the question for someone who has never opened the app: what the three
sections are, that the path is scales → notation → hymns, the mode order (Pl. 4 → 1 → Pl. 1 → 4,
then 2 / Pl. 2, then 3 / Grave), and one concrete first step (open Scales → Pl. 4, *play*, then
*tune*). Home links to it. A *Beginner* tag sits on Pl. 4 and on the simplest hymns (Holy Holy
Holy now); Papadic pieces carry *not for beginners* once any exist. No gates.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hand the phone to someone who has not seen the app; they reach the Pl. 4 ladder from *Where do
  I start?* without help. The operator may stand in for this person.
- The page names the mode order and the hymn tiers in plain English and fits in about one phone
  screen of text.
- Pl. 4 and Holy Holy Holy show the *Beginner* tag; nothing is locked or hidden.

## HITL / AFK

AFK; the operator reads it once.

## Constraints

- No gates, streaks, points or progress bars. Signposting is tags and hints only.
- The page text lives in a Markdown or data file, not in a component, so the operator can edit
  it without touching code.
- Hymn tiers for reference: (1) Divine Liturgy fixed hymns, each in its most common setting;
  (2) the eight Resurrectional apolytikia; (3) fixed Vespers/Orthros pieces, including Κύριε
  ἐκέκραξα in its eight modes. Papadic pieces (Cherubic, Communion) are carried with *not for
  beginners*, not excluded.

## Blockers

- 04, 06 — the tags need pages to sit on. The page text itself can be written any time after 01.
